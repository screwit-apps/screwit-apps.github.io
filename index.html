Support

Bug, question, or idea — write us.

support@screwitapps.com

Please include:

  • The app name (for example, Truco, Generala & Yatzy, or Carioca)
  • Your device model and OS version (iOS or Android)
  • The app version (in Settings or About)
  • What happened, and how to reproduce it if you can

We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

FAQ

Are the apps free?

Yes. Free to download and use. Some features may be supported by optional ads.

How do I save a game?

In scorekeeper apps, use Save during play. Come back later and continue where you left off.

Where are past games?

Open History from the home screen to review completed matches.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. No account is required.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@screwitapps.com with the app name, device, version, and what happened. Screenshots help.

Can I request a feature?

Yes — write to support@screwitapps.com. We read every message.
