Delete your Itinerary account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Itinerary account and the trip data stored for that account on our servers.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Itinerary on your Android device and sign in with Google.
  2. On the My trips screen, tap the Account icon in the top app bar.
  3. Scroll to the session section and tap Delete account (Spanish UI: Borrar cuenta).
  4. Confirm when prompted. You will be signed out after the request is accepted.

Depending on server configuration, deletion may take effect immediately or on a scheduled date shown in the app response. Your account should not remain usable for creating or syncing new trips after a successful deletion request.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify account-linked data on the Itinerary backend, including:

  • Your account profile (for example email address, display name, and avatar URL if stored)
  • Trip preferences associated with your account
  • Your trips and generated itineraries stored for your account
  • Authentication sessions and refresh tokens for that account

What we may keep (limited time)

Some data may be retained for a limited period for security, legal compliance, or backup recovery, then removed according to our retention practices.

Crash and analytics data processed by Google Firebase (where enabled) may be retained under Google’s retention rules. Advertising partners may retain data under their own policies if you interacted with ads in the app.

Local data on your device (such as cached tokens) is removed when you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email support@screwitapps.com from the same email address linked to your Google sign-in. Use the subject line Itinerary account deletion. We will verify ownership and process the request.
